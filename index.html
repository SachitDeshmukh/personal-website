Victim

I am tempted to move my hands. The worry is making my palms sweat, my hands tremble. I prudently move my hand towards those steady eyes and hover above them in apprehension. The moonlight is reaching their eyes no more, and what lurks, is the shadow of my iniquitous ambitions.
