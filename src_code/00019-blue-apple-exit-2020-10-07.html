The Blue Apple

You take the ascending stairs.

07 OCTOBER 2020 • Sachit Deshmukh • Resetting your timer

You ascend the stairs, taking you to a door. Could this be the door to the storage facility? There is a guard standing beside it, protecting the electronically locked door. The guard looks at you, inspects you.

“Do you have it?” he asks.

You give it a thought, and ultimately affirm that you have it. Without saying a word, the guard opens the door for you. You take a step.

There is an extremely bright light that shines on your eyes out of the blue. You realize you have left the laboratory. You are in a world outside the laboratory.

You wonder what was out of the windows in that case. You turn to look at this new world, this being the first time you witness it first hand. The door you got out of was the way to the underground.

All this time you were under the earth, like an earthworm, finding its way up. The voice in your head is no more audible. The contact with the laboratory seems to have been disabled. You look around you.

Nothing what you expected. It barely matches the description the disks in the laboratory gave you about this world. But it does not bother you. You feel there's a better disk in your system right now.

There's only this one thing that still bothers you. Nothing has color. You look at the sky, the individuals running past you, the dogs barking at you, the birds flying about, the cleaning bots sweeping the roads at every stop sign, the damn stop signs, but all is still black and white.

What bothers you more is that you can tell which color every single object is, but you cannot experience it. You turn back to open the door to the laboratory, but there is nothing behind you. A big playground, full of younger individuals playing with machines.

The laboratory is nowhere to be found. This is your life now. Whether you have your answers or not. You look back at the streets, the buildings, the advertisements, the new life you are about to embark on. A billboard across the street reads “You are always ready!”.
