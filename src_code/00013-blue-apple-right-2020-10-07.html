The Blue Apple

You take a right.

07 OCTOBER 2020 • Sachit Deshmukh • 10 mins left

You take a right and start walking through the passage having closed doors. Behind each door probably exist individuals in rooms similar to the one you have. Afraid that might trigger more alarms, you refrain from opening any other door.

The noise that was previously audible has somehow stopped. The flashing light seems to have silenced. All you see is this passage, endless, with countless doors on each side. On each door, there hangs a plate, on which is written a combination of numbers and letters similar to the one written on your door. Nothing unusual so far.

That color, how did you know it was red? Why did it look like any other color? You start reading the plates on the doors. FJNK 1984, FJNK 1993, FJNK 2000, FJNK 2020, FJNK 2035.

Starting from number 2100, the plates are peculiarly colored. Your brain can make out what color it is, but you cannot distinguish one plate from the other just by its appearance.

What could have possibly gone wrong? You did everything mentioned in the instruction manual of the disk. Nothing should have gone wrong. Unfortunately, it has. And you have no idea how to fix it. You are just going where the way leads you.

You have been walking for about half an hour now. “Will this passage lead you anywhere?” you start doubting. You read each plate to make sure you are not going in circles. No. You have not been. Each plate reads a unique combination. But these combinations never seem to end.

Your pace increases with each step. You now ignore the plates on the doors and start focusing on the light coming from the end of the passage. You start running towards it. Passing doors are now labeled differently.

“Storage” reads one door, but you run past it. Your gaze fixed at that light which never seems to be near. You start dashing with long strides. Using everything you have got, exhausting yourself.

The remote source of light teases you. After a point, the doors seem to disappear, and only a wall stands beside you. You decide to never stop running. At one moment you lower your pace to regain some energy and your body collapses. You cannot move your hands, your legs, or even your face.

You are simply lying in the middle of an infinite passage. Stuck. Immobile. Clueless. Unanswered. Incomplete.

A voice can be heard yet again.

“Sample FJNK one nine eight two has been successfully shut down. Scrap it and replace it with the next subject. This new one will not expe--rien---ce----”

Start Over.
