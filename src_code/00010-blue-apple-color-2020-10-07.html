The Blue Apple

You can see color.

07 OCTOBER 2020 • Sachit Deshmukh • Undefined time left

An apple in rainbow colors. Colors. Seven of the many perceptible colors. You feel an overwhelming tension in your head.

You have known every single fact one could fathom about colors. And yet the image seems to give you something more to know. The image soon fades and then gradually each and every single object around you reveals its color.

You had once known every corner of the place you lived in, but now you feel a strong urge to go run around and see everything anew, from a different perspective, with this new knowledge of color.

The first thing that you see is yourself. It is not a color you expected, never anticipated, but it suits you very well. You look at the bed you were seated on, the walls around you, the floor tiles, the disk collection you have kept neatly in the cupboard.

You look at the sky through the window. A hue decorated with silhouettes of lush green trees against the setting sun. You go unraveling the rest of the place with eager eyes, letting each color seep into you, immersing yourself into the shades and tints, swirling into the soft pastels.

You know you can never get enough of this. Just then, you see a strong red light cover the entire place briefly, and then fade away, only to repeat this numerous times until a voice of panic can be heard.

“Sample FJNK one nine eight three has seen color!”

You hear that same voice repeat itself twice more, and then a sudden thud on a wall. Before you know, the wall breaks into pieces, and some individuals barge into the room.

“You know. That's not good. Shut the system down.”
