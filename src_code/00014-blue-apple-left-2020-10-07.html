The Blue Apple

You take a left.

07 OCTOBER 2020 • Sachit Deshmukh • Undefined time left

You take a left. The hallway which was once full of individuals going about their work now has noticed you. Each and every individual is looking at you like watching a wild animal out of its cage. You don't have time to pay attention to their piercing glares.

You start rushing through the hallway, trying to find an exit. Brushing past bodies, covering your eyes from the intensifying flashing red light, ignoring the voice. The more you run, the louder the voice gets.

You are getting closer to the voice. And then you pass the last open door, ending up facing a wall. You turn back and are prepared to sprint back to your cell. But the path is blocked by the same individuals. Only this time, it is not only eyes that are pointing at you.

One of the individuals is holding a flat object at you, their thumb lying on a switch. You strike out the possibility of running past them. Discreetly, you look to your left and right. To your right is just a plain wall, but on your left lies a room with its door open.

Chilly air is coming from the room. Without giving it much thought you rush into the room, closing the door shut behind you. Knocks on the door are immediately heard. It won't take them much time to break into the room.

You quickly scan the insides of this room. It is not much different from the one you grew up in. Except that it is extremely dark, and unbearably cold. The floors are covered in a mist, giving an impression of standing on clouds. There must be a window somewhere you think to yourself.

Right in the center of the room, there is a glass cylinder, suspended mid-air. You approach it and find out that the object inside it is labeled "H.E.A.R.T.". From the corner of your eye, you see a manual, presumably on the usage of this device.

You pick it up and start skimming through it. You hear a few more knocks. From the manual, you get to know that this is an organ found in a species known as humans. Installing it is very easy. Just when you are about to read the instructions, you hear the individuals break into the room.

You start running. You install the HEART.
