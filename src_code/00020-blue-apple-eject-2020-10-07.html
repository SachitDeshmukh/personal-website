The Blue Apple

You take the descending stairs.

07 OCTOBER 2020 • Sachit Deshmukh • 5 mins left

You descend the stairs, and they lead you to a door. This could be the door to the storage you think. You try the lock, and it is not locked. You open the door, and a passage is revealed. Chilly air is coming out of the passage, the mist on the floor is reminiscent of the room you once entered to get a heart.

“The storage facility is very unique when it comes to its appearance. Tall shelves, long columns, endless rows, nothing like that. It does not look like a storage room at all. What you will see there, I can't tell you, because you will see what you have been programmed to see.”

At the end of the passage, there is another door. This one though is locked. A hollow cylinder hands to its right. Under the cylinder is a button, probably a switch that opens the door. You press the button, but nothing happens.

You look at the cylinder, and it reminds you of something. You try to think about what exactly it could contain. The door, the chilled air, the mist, the cylinder - THE HEART.

You place your hand where the heart has been installed, and gently press the eject button on it. The heart slips out effortlessly, and lands in your hands. Without much hesitation, you place it in the cylinder, press the button, and watch as the door opens.

To your horror, the door did not lead you to the storage facility, but instead in the hands of the very individuals you were running away from. They are right there, closing in on you, entrapping you, just like the heart that could have saved you your life.
