The Blue Apple

You continue reading after seeing color.

07 OCTOBER 2020 • Sachit Deshmukh • 5 mins left

Three individuals, all wearing white lab coats are standing right before you. They seem to have the look of disappointment on their faces. As though you failed them. You did something that you were not supposed to do. And for that, it seems, they want to punish you.

“You know. That's not good,” says one of them, seemingly the head of the group, “shut the system down,” they order the other two. The individuals start approaching you, trying to reach for your hands. You start running across the passage into your bedroom.

“There's no use trying. You can't run away.”

You close the door behind you as you enter your bedroom. There is not much time to think. You need an exit.

“We shut'em down with or without the memory. Take out the device.” You hear them whisper.

You've got to make a quick move. They bang on the door once, and your gaze shifts to the window. No sooner do they break in, than you climb out of the window and jump.

“Nice try,” the head individual says with a smirk, as they take out the device, and press a simple switch pointing at you. Everything seems to stop for that one instant. You find yourself floating mid-air. Not falling down, not rising up. Just rotating in that one spot.

You seem to be floating in what looks like clouds, black clouds coming out of chimneys of factories. From the corner of your eye, you can see the three individuals leaving the room, removing any trace of your existence, taking away your disks, and repairing any damage they caused to the room. They are trying to erase the fact that you existed. And just like a simple thought, they forget you.

You try to swim back to the room, but you feel a pullback from something you are affixed to. Every single skill you had, starts to fade away. Your memories seem out of place, not making any sense to you. Your body parts start dismantling, floating about in the clouds like individual machines.

Every single thing turns back to either a black or a white. You forget what are color receptors, what are wavelengths. You forget what is light. You see yourself fading into the clouds as you forget you are alive. What exists of you is only in particles that float along with the clouds, getting carried away by the winds.
