Leaving Home, Entering Classrooms: Traveling for Work in 2023, 2024

From Pune and Delhi to Lucknow, Koderma, and Bangalore.

01 JUNE 2025 • Sachit Deshmukh • 5 min read

I am 6 months late in admitting this: 2024 pushed me to explore my curiosity.

— me, in awe and excitement!

Since 2023, I have witnessed myself step out of my comfort zone almost every other month for a minimum of 2 weeks each time. I visited Delhi, Bangalore, Lucknow, and Koderma for the first time in my life. I went to numerous government schools, classrooms, and Anganwadis, where the teachers and students warmly welcomed me despite not speaking my language.

I met passionate people, striving to make an impact on the nation's education outcomes. If you told 2022-Sachit that they would not be in Pune for more than 5 months of the year, it would have felt like a joke to them. It had felt impossible to be on my own, explore new cities, and constantly interact with strangers.

Throughout 2023 and 2024, my work at J-PAL South Asia pushed me to interact with new people on a regular basis to ensure the good work reaches the intended beneficiaries.

It involved countless interactions on project progress, challenges and hiccups, and logistical arrangements. I engaged in brainstorming sessions to think futuristic, to strategize, to take input and feedback to update plans, and execute them. It made me draw insights and learnings from field data, conversations with civil society organizations, anecdotes and experiences of beneficiaries, and crucial feedback.

There I was in 2023 and 2024, experiencing varied perspectives, exploring new environments, and witnessing grassroots-level change that I could contribute to. It did not, and still does not, feel real.

I have too many stories and conversations that I hold close. Each engagement contributes to this "core island" in my mind. As I continue my work with J-PAL South Asia, I aspire to keep an open mind to the upcoming experiences. It has gotten me here. It will take me further ahead.
