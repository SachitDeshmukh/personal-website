The Blue Apple

You installed the HEART.

07 OCTOBER 2020 • Sachit Deshmukh • Undefined time left

You haphazardly put the heart in the place you think it is supposed to be in. You turn around to face the individuals, determined to shut your system down.

For a moment you doubt your choice. Maybe making a run for a window was a better option. Surprisingly, as though you were invisible, the individuals make no move, and simply exit the room they broke into.

“A human would not do any harm to the experiment,” you hear one mention to the other, relieved to have not had an error in the said experiment.

You don't understand it. You take the manual and decide to go through it carefully this time.

“Human Energy and Reactions Transmitter, H-E-A-R-T.”

Just when you pick it up, a familiar disk drops from it. You grab hold of the disk and read the inscription on it. The audio format of the manual. To be used by non-human individuals.

Non-human. You have never heard of this word before. You think that this disk may shed some light upon it, and immediately put it into your body through your head. After about a few seconds, a voice starts to speak.

“A human energy and reactions transmitter. This is the most delicate device of them all. Please avoid usage to any cost unless prescribed by an authorized human.”

You start moving out of the room, leaving the cold atmosphere behind you. Your eyes scan the surroundings yet again.

“This disk is developed to guide an individual who is either about to install the heart or who is experiencing the changes this heart is designed to make. In simple words, you are now a human. At least the humans and individuals around you will look at you as a human. You are no longer a subject of an experiment. You will be treated as a human, as an experimenter. Your core fundamentals are not going to change. You still are an individual anatomically. You will only have the powers a human can have in this laboratory.”

As you walk through the hallway back to your room, not a single individual notices you. They carry on with their research as though nothing happened, as though no anomaly existed in their experiment. You wonder if any of them are just like you, individuals with a human heart.

“Everything is going to be different for you from now on. As just an individual, you were programmed to carry out some functions, test out new discoveries, you were monitored for anything you did. Your life was restricted to your respective cell. Whatever you got to know was through such similar disks. You have lived a life that was determined. Any choice you thought you made was made for you. This heart was installed within you for a purpose that will be soon clear to you.”

You, however, are eyeing the individuals with the most piercing of eyes, studying everyone carefully, making sure no one is questioning you.

“This heart changes everything. You will now know freedom known to no other individual without a heart. You will be in control of your life. You can move about without any restrictions in the entire laboratory.”

You walk up to your room in perfect condition. Safe and sound. The voice from the disk still talking to you.

“From this point onwards, all decisions you make will be yours. The laboratory is yours to explore. There are other individuals you can study, how they react to the new stimuli they are exposed to. You will get first-hand knowledge by observing them and discussing it with the other humans. Priceless knowledge. You can always go back to your room and try to get answers through more disks. I should warn you, if you go back to your room, the doors will be shut for the rest of your life. But the information library you can now access being a human is immense. If you were to realize that there were any defects in your body, you will now be able to fix them.”

The voice stops. You can see the passage straight ahead, full of closed doors. You give one final look at your room. This is where it all started.

You walk straight ahead. You enter your room.
