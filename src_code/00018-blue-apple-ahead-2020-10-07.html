The Blue Apple

You walk straight ahead.

07 OCTOBER 2020 • Sachit Deshmukh • Undefined time left

One step at a time, you start walking through the hallway. To your left and right are doors. All having labels similar to the one you saw on your door. Probably each door contains an individual being monitored.

The amount of doors freaks you out. Each door means that there is an experiment going on right now on an individual, who unfortunately does not know that they can escape, get a heart of their own and run away from this laboratory.

“Good choice,” the voice from the disk materializes in your head, “I knew you would want to explore this great laboratory. Now allow me to guide you through it.”

“To your right are individuals who will experience color after the color-vision disk. To your left are those who will not. The question is whether they will consider it new information or…”

Their voice trails off as you begin to ponder upon your situation. You were supposed to see color, and yet something stops you from being able to. There might be somewhere in the laboratory where you can find more disks regarding this.

“For starters, you will have no access to your room anymore. Let's just consider it to be demolished. What you are witnessing right now is the key experimenting unit. It is an endless space containing countless cells of infinite individuals, each representing one possibility of them responding to their surroundings. Each individual is unique. One might see color in their early research years, some might have to wait until the last functioning second of their system.”

Having paid attention to the voice, you wonder what happens to individuals who never see color in spite of being programmed to. You eventually reach the end of the hallway. With no way forward, you look sideways and find out that each way has a staircase.

“The other floors have humans monitoring every move an individual makes, every skill they learn, every mechanism they develop. There is a floor in this laboratory where an uncountable number of disks are stored. Each unit is numbered according to research-years. Not the years it took to create that disk, but the average time period when individuals ask for that specific disk. No disk is ever invented. That storage facility contains every single skill disk ever known to our kind. The location of the facility constantly changes, presumably because some individual finds out about it.”

One staircase going downward. The other upward. Either could lead you to the storage.

You take the ascending stairs. You take the descending stairs.
