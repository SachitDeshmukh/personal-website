The Blue Apple

You decide to make a run.

07 OCTOBER 2020 • Sachit Deshmukh • 5 mins left

You don't have the time to install a new device. You make a run in search of a window. You take a left from the living room into the hallway and keep running. The cold air makes it harder to breathe.

You feel the sweat dripping off your face. Marching footsteps can be heard, progressively getting closer to you. The hallway does not seem to end. You have been running for quite a while now, there has not been one room that you have passed by.

Your legs feel tired. You can't lift them for one more step. You stop running. You are gasping for fresh air. You rest your hand on the wall, and your body instantaneously collapses on the floor.

The mist circles around your body. You can still hear the rushing footsteps in the background. With every passing second, the source seems to be closer. You throw a glance at the hallway behind you. To your horror, the sound of the footsteps is no longer audible.

You can't seem to feel your body. You don't struggle for any air. The mist around you no longer feels cold. You cannot process any information it is receiving from the other body parts.

Soon the parts stop functioning. You can't tell if you are asleep or just shutting down. Everything seems to be in a trance. Nothing makes sense anymore. You stay in this frozen state while some individuals circle you, dismantling every little bit of your existence.

“Found the disk.”

“Destroy it right away.”

“Have a look at this. The eyes don't have color receptors installed.”
