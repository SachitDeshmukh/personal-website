The Blue Apple

You cannot see color.

07 OCTOBER 2020 • Sachit Deshmukh • Undefined time left

An apple with black and white strips. You are confused. This disk was supposed to give you the ability of color perception, the ultimate step in understanding the human eye. But somehow, there seems to be an error with the disk, and it has not given you that skill.

You get mad at the disk being faulty. This has never happened. Every disk has been flawless, improving you as an individual, giving you skills that not all individuals possess. You have never had to report a bug in any of the disks. You look at the disk collection placed in the cupboard.

None of them have flaws. You have no idea what is to be done in such a situation. You look around your room, no device to communicate with anyone. You have never had to communicate with any other individual before.

For the first time, you feel a drop of sweat drip down your face. You get out of your bedroom and step into the living room. There are no hints of a door being around. You have never needed one. It hits you that there has been one window in your bedroom. You run back into your bedroom, approach the window, and push to open it. Nothing happens.

You pull the window but to no avail. You try lifting it, and yet nothing happens. You will have to break it perhaps. You run into the living room again and start searching for any sharp or huge object. Nothing. The place suddenly feels empty. Not a single object that could help you get out of this place is lying around.

You start doubting whether someone is watching you from somewhere, placing only the required objects in the room, and deleting objects that could remotely give you any control over the situation. You start pressing your palms on the walls, trying to find a secret wall or opening of some sort.

Something seems to be pushed near the huge painting of a fruit basket. Convenient. The painting which once was full of colors now had lost all of them. When you were painting it, you had no idea what each color would look like on the canvas, you only had the instructions to paint the appropriate one in the respective spot.

And now, you would never see those colors. You take the painting down. And start pushing around the marks it has left. A portal of the wall gives way. And to your relief, there is an opening.

You take the first step out of the only room you have ever lived in. You are eager to see the outside world, the individuals that live there, those who interact with each other on a daily basis. You anticipate to see a bustling street, to see the cars of the honks you hear every morning before you wake up, to see other houses like yours.

But what you see is not what you expected it to be. You find yourself standing in the middle of a long, endless hallway, having doors with labels on them. You read the label on your door. It reads FJNK-1982.

Suddenly, a flashing light enters your eyes and you hear a panicky voice saying that "sample FJNK one nine eight two is out of the cell". That's you!

You look around for anyone coming at you. To your left are individuals running in and out of other such opened doors, operating on their virtual notepads, interacting, sharing some piece of information, and looks of horror on their faces.

To your right, the hallway is empty. The doors on this side of the hallway are still closed, showing no signs of activity inside them whatsoever. The weirdest thing is that your brain registers the light around you as the color red. But it looks the same to you as any other.

“Sample FJNK one nine eight three is out of the cell. Find it, RIGHT NOW!” shouts a voice, but you are unable to quite put from which side of the hallway.

“Find it and shut it down!”

You have to move quickly.

Take a left. Take a right.
